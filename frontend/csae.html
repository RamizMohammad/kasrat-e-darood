Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 29 June 2026

Kasrat-e-Darood ("we", "us", "the app") has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page sets out our published standards for preventing, detecting, and responding to such content and conduct, in line with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy and applicable law.

1. Our commitment

Kasrat-e-Darood is a faith-based community recitation tracker intended for a general audience. We are committed to keeping our service free of any content or behavior that sexualizes, exploits, endangers, or otherwise harms children. CSAE and CSAM are strictly prohibited.

2. Prohibited content and conduct

The following are strictly forbidden on our app and services:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form — images, video, audio, text, or links.
  • Sexualization of minors, including sexualized depictions, descriptions, or commentary.
  • Grooming, solicitation, sextortion, or any attempt to contact or exploit a minor.
  • Trafficking, advertising, or facilitating access to CSAE/CSAM.
  • Normalizing, promoting, or encouraging the sexual abuse of children.

3. How we prevent CSAE

  • Limited user content. The app is built around personal/community recitation counts and does not provide open public messaging, media uploads, or anonymous content sharing between strangers. The only user-provided media is a personal profile photo and an account display name.
  • Account requirements. Use of the app requires an authenticated account (email or Google sign-in).
  • Moderation of contributable content. Library content (recitations and categories) can only be added by designated trusted roles (super members / admins), not by the general public.
  • Standards enforcement. Profile names and photos that violate these standards are removed, and the responsible accounts are suspended or banned.

4. Reporting abuse

If you encounter content or behavior that may involve the sexual abuse or exploitation of a child on our service, please report it to us immediately:

Child-safety contact: kasratedarood@gmail.com (please include "CHILD SAFETY" in the subject line).

Reports are treated as urgent and confidential. We aim to acknowledge and begin reviewing every child-safety report promptly.

5. How we respond

  • We promptly review every report and remove violating content.
  • We suspend or permanently ban accounts involved in CSAE/CSAM.
  • We preserve relevant data as permitted by law to support investigations.
  • We report apparent CSAM to the appropriate authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or local law enforcement, as required by applicable law.

6. Reporting to authorities & resources

Suspected child exploitation can also be reported directly to:

  • NCMEC CyberTipline: report.cybertip.org
  • India: National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal — cybercrime.gov.in, or call the Childline helpline at 1098.
  • Emergencies: contact your local police or emergency services.

7. Compliance with Google Play

These standards are published in accordance with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy. We comply with all applicable laws regarding CSAE and CSAM and cooperate with lawful requests from platforms and authorities.

8. Contact

For any questions about these standards, contact us at kasratedarood@gmail.com.
